## Why “nothing happened”

The script only considers **reversal breakout** setups that also pass this filter:

- **Daily volume filter:** At the time of the potential entry bar, **today’s cumulative volume (so far)** must already be **greater than the full previous day’s volume**.
  - So for 23 Feb, volume from 09:15 up to that bar must exceed total volume of 22 Feb.
  - This often isn’t true until later in the day, so many valid-looking breakouts get dropped.

So one or both of these is true for 2026-02-23:

1. **No reversal breakout pattern was found** for any of the 18 symbols (no sharp move ≥4%, pullback to 20 EMA, then break above consolidation with green entry candle, within SL/range/pullback/consolidation limits).
2. **One or more breakouts were found** but **all were filtered out** because, at that bar’s time, today’s volume so far was not yet above the full previous day’s volume.
